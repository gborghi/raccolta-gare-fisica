The figure shows a compressed spring between two strollers tied by a wire. The wagons are initially stationary on a horizontal plane without friction. Carriage A weighs 2 kg, carriage B weighs 1 kg.

• What happens when the wire is cut and the wagons move away in opposite directions?

- **A.** The time interval during which force acts on cart A is twice that during which force acts on cart B.
- **B.** The force intensity acting on cart A is half the force intensity acting on cart B.
- **C.** The force pulse acting on cart A is twice the force pulse acting on cart B.
- **D.** The kinetic energy purchased from cart A is twice the energy purchased from cart B.
- **E.** The acceleration of cart A is half the acceleration of cart B.
