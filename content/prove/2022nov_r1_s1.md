The Stefan-Boltzmann law says that the emitted power of a spherical "black body", $P$, is related to the radius, $R$, and absolute surface temperature, $T$, as $P \propto R^2 T^4$. Wien's "displacement law" says that the wavelength $\lambda_{\max}$ corresponding to the peak value of the emitted power of this spectrum is inversely proportional to the absolute surface temperature. The Sun currently has its peak wavelength as 500 nm.

What will be the new peak wavelength when it becomes a red giant, given its radius will be 200 times larger and its power output 4000 times larger?
