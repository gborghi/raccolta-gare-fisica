(Units are given in brackets)

Beats are produced when two waves of similar frequencies interfere, causing slowly varying constructive and destructive interference. If the frequencies differ by 1 Hz then the constructive to destructive to constructive interference will occur once each second, giving a beat frequency of 1 Hz.

A siren producing a note of 256 Hz and an organ pipe of slightly lower frequency full of air at 17°C, when played together give a beat frequency of 4 Hz. On slowly changing the temperature of the air in the pipe, the beat frequency first reduces to zero and then increases again to 4 Hz. What is the change in temperature of the air in the pipe?

The speed of a wave in air is given by $v = \sqrt{\dfrac{\gamma P}{\rho}}$ where $\gamma$ is a numerical constant, $P$ is the pressure and $\rho$ is the density of the air.
