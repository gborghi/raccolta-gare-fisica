**a)** Physicists sometimes use the approximation that light travels in a vacuum at a speed of 1 foot in 1 ns. What is the percentage error in using this value?
(1.000 m = 1.094 yards and 1.000 yard = 3.000 feet) **[3]**

**Risposta:** 1 foot/ns $=3.047\times10^8$ m s$^{-1}$; error $=\dfrac{3.047-3.000}{3.000}=1.564\%=1.6\%$ (using $1-0.9846=1.54\%$ gains only 2/3 marks)

**d)** A student standing at a distance $a$ from a vertical wall kicks a ball from ground level with velocity $V$ at an angle $\alpha$ to the horizontal in a plane perpendicular to that of the wall. The ball strikes the wall and rebounds. The coefficient of restitution for the collision is $e=2/3$. The ball first strikes the ground at a distance $2a$ from the wall. $e$ is the ratio of the components of velocity at normal incidence to the wall, before and after collision; $e=\dfrac{v_{after}}{v_{before}}\le1$.
Find $a$ in terms of $V$, $\alpha$ and $g$, the gravitational field strength. **[6]**

**Risposta:** horizontal speed $V\cos\alpha$ before, $eV\cos\alpha$ after; $t_2=\tfrac{2}{e}t_1=3t_1$; vertical motion unaffected, $t_1+t_2=\dfrac{2V\sin\alpha}{g}$: $V^2\sin2\alpha=\left(\tfrac{2}{e}+1\right)ga=4ga$, i.e. $a=\dfrac{V^2\sin2\alpha}{4g}$

**e)** A helicopter of total mass 1000 kg is able to remain in a stationary position by imparting a uniform downward velocity to a cylinder of air below it of effective diameter 6 m. Assuming the density of air to be 1.2 kg m$^{-3}$, calculate the downward velocity of the air. **[5]**

**g)** In a factory heating system, water enters the radiators at 60 °C and leaves at 38 °C. **r)** A pond is covered by a layer of ice 5 cm thick. How long will it be before the ice is 10 cm thick if the air temperature stays constant at −10 °C?
Assume the density of ice = 900 kg m$^{-3}$; the latent heat of fusion of ice = 330 kJ kg$^{-1}$; the thermal conductivity of ice = 2.1 W m$^{-1}$ K$^{-1}$.
The power flowing perpendicular to the faces through a uniform material is given by power flow $P=\lambda A\dfrac{(T_H-T_C)}{x}$, in which $\lambda$ is the thermal conductivity of the material, $T_H$ is the hotter temperature at one face of the material, $T_C$ is the colder temperature on the other face, $A$ is the area of a face, and $x$ is the thickness of the material. **[7]**

**Risposta:** $\rho AL\dfrac{\Delta x}{\Delta t}=\lambda A\dfrac{T_H-T_C}{x}$; integrating, $t_2-t_1=\dfrac{L\rho}{2\lambda}\dfrac{x_2^2-x_1^2}{T_H-T_C}=\dfrac{330\times10^3\times900\times75\times10^{-4}}{4.2\times10}=53.0\times10^3$ s $=884$ minutes $=14.7$ hours
