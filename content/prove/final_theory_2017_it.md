 We denote the spaceship's reference frame by $\Sigma'$. All dimensions that are measured by the control tower relative to $\Sigma'$ are indicated with a prime. The cockpit of the spaceship is located at the tip, and serves as the origin of the reference frame $\Sigma'$.
