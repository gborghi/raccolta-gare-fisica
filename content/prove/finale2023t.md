**Bicchiere d'acqua nell'oceano**

Egli riempie una seconda volta il bicchiere con la stessa quantità d'acqua e si chiede quale sia il numero $N$ di molecole che sono state pescate entrambe le volte nel bicchiere. Quanto vale $\log_{10} N$?

**Turn of death of a rigid body**

A spherical object makes a death spin on a circular $R = 5.0\ \text{m}$ radius support, as shown in Figure 1. The support is fixed to the ground. The object is launched at the base of the circumference with an initial velocity of $v_0 = 12\ \text{m/s}$ (tangential, hence horizontal) and rolls without cracking from the initial moment. The object has spherical symmetry, mass $M = 2.0\ \text{kg}$ and radius $r = 50\ \text{cm}$, but non-uniform density: its moment of inertia is $\beta M r^2$, where $\beta$ is a real number. What is the minimum value of $\beta$ that allows the sphere to complete a complete death spin without detachment from the wheel?
