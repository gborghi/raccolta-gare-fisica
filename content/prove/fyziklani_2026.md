**AA — Summer drink**

Consider a cylindrical glass with an internal radius of $R = 3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ and an ice cube with a side length of $a = 2.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ floating on the surface. The height of the liquid level, including the floating ice, is $h = 10\,\mathrm{cm}$. By how many centimeters will the level change once the ice cube has completely melted? Provide a positive value if the level increases and a negative value if it decreases.

**Risposta:** $\Delta h = 0\,\mathrm{cm}$ (the level does not change)

**AB — Marek's ride**

A sightseeing tram T3 Coupé equipped with turbo engines is racing through the streets at breakneck speed. Behind the controls sits nobody other than he. The man. The myth. The legend. Marek Milička. With every passing second, he approaches a collapsed bridge over the Vltava river, which has a width of $d = 200\,\mathrm{m}$. The remains of the bridge consist of ramps on both banks, which form an angle of $\vartheta = 10^\circ$ with the ground. Marek accelerates and… he jumps over the river with the tram, landing successfully on the ramp on the opposite bank. At what speed was Marek's tram moving immediately before the jump?

**Risposta:** $v = \sqrt{gd/\sin 2\vartheta} \approx 75.7\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}} \approx 272.7\,\mathrm{km\,h^{-1}}$

**AH — Water in the air**

A firefighter is extinguishing a fire by continuously spraying the surroundings with a steady stream of water from a hose from an elevated position. The hose is held so that the nozzle is at a height $h_0 = 3.2\,\mathrm{m}$ above the ground, onto which the water subsequently falls. Water flows out of the hose of diameter $d = 75\,\mathrm{mm}$ with speed $v_0 = 4.2\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ at an initial angle $\alpha = 35^\circ$ upward relative to the horizontal plane. What is the mass of water that is present in the air at any given moment?

**Risposta:** $M = \dfrac{\pi d^2 \rho v_0}{4g}\left(v_0\sin\alpha + \sqrt{v_0^2\sin^2\alpha + 2gh_0}\right) \approx 20\,\mathrm{kg}$
