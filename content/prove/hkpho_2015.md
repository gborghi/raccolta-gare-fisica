- **(B)** $N_1 = \dfrac{1}{2}\left(mg + \dfrac{hmv^2}{ar}\right)$, $N_2 = \dfrac{1}{2}\left(mg - \dfrac{hmv^2}{ar}\right)$.

(c) Find the total time duration that the ball travels. Express your answer in terms of $H_o$ and $C_R$.

Hints: You may assume:
i. $C_R$ is constant throughout all the bouncing,
