Problem 3 (10 points)
Helicopter on Mars
On 19 April 2021, a human-developed aircraft took off on Mars for the first time. The
Ingenuity helicopter has since completed numerous flights in the Martian atmosphere. The
two counter-rotating rotors of the aircraft have a diameter of 1.2 m and the
total mass of the helicopter is about 1.8 kg.
During the development of Ingenuity on Earth, the conditions in the Martian atmosphere had to be taken into account. On Earth, whose atmosphere near the ground has a density of
1.2 kg $m-3$, the helicopter took off at 500 rotor revolutions per minute. The density
of the Martian atmosphere near the ground, by contrast, is only about 0.020 kg $m-3$. Mars has
a diameter of about 6.8 $\cdot$ 103 km and a mass of approximately 6.4 $\cdot$ 1023 kg
Using the given data, determine the gravitational acceleration on Mars. Estimate
how many revolutions per minute the rotors of Ingenuity must turn on Mars at minimum for the helicopter to take off.

Questo fenomeno è dovuto alle oscillazioni dell'aria nel collo della bottiglia. La frequenza f di queste oscillazioni dipende dal volume d'aria al di sotto del collo della bottiglia, nella parte ampia della bottiglia, e per volumi non troppo piccoli è approssimativamente data da

Inoltre, ̃L corrisponde alla lunghezza corretta del collo della bottiglia, che tiene conto del fatto che la pressione dell'aria al bordo superiore del collo non scende immediatamente al valore della pressione esterna. Puoi assumere che ̃L $\approx L+\pi$ 4 d, dove L indica la lunghezza del collo della bottiglia e d il suo diametro interno.

In questo esperimento devi studiare la frequenza del tono prodotto in funzione del volume d'aria presente nella bottiglia.

Per questo hai bisogno di una bottiglia cilindrica in vetro con un collo il più dritto possibile (ad esempio di una bibita o del vino), una bilancia da cucina o un misurino il più preciso possibile, acqua e un metro rigido. Per determinare la frequenza del tono prodotto puoi, ad esempio, utilizzare la funzione "Audio Autocorrelation" dell'app phyphox, che visualizza direttamente la frequenza di un tono.

Tuttavia, per rilevare meglio le deviazioni sistematiche e impedire che i singoli valori con grandi errori abbiano un'influenza eccessiva, è consigliabile linearizzare il rapporto dato tra la frequenza f e il volume V e valutarlo graficamente.

Show your talent!

Students
If you are a student, the IPhO and the Physics Olympiad in Germany offer you diverse opportunities to engage intensively with physics problems, to experience physics as an exciting scientific discipline, to test your own limits, and not least to meet interesting people.

For the competition rounds there are learning materials and training problems that help you deepen your knowledge and problem-solving skills. At the seminars you meet many other young people enthusiastic about physics.

Taking part is therefore worthwhile in any case, regardless of whether you make it to the higher rounds. What matters is to be there. Successfully completing the first round is already a special achievement and a real distinction.

So, take heart!

Teachers
As a teacher, you can offer a challenge in physics to particularly capable or interested students with the problems of the Physics Olympiad and encourage them to engage more deeply with physics topics. The Physics Olympiad can thus serve as an instrument of individual support.

The problems of the 1st round in particular are suitable not only for the best in a class.

With a wide range of offerings, the
PhysicsOlympiad aims to broadly reach interested young people and inspire them permanently for the natural sciences. To this end, support programs such as the Orpheus seminars and accompanying materials for the first round are provided, designed to assist you in introducing PhysicsOlympiad topics.

Therefore, please encourage your students to participate; only those who do not take part can lose.

Schools
By encouraging participation in competitions, schools can enhance their profile and use these events, in the sense of enrichment, as a complement to regular school offerings. Competitions provide diverse and differentiated learning environments for participating students.

In the field of STEM subjects, the Olympiads—especially in later rounds—represent a competition aimed at particularly motivated and high-achieving young people. Nevertheless, participation is worthwhile even in the initial rounds and can also foster lasting enthusiasm for STEM topics. Programs such as the Orpheus seminars allow support for a large number of participants.

In many federal states, participation may additionally be recognised as a special academic achievement or subject/seminar paper for your students’ Abitur.

Interested in more than physics?
The IPhO is one of the six nationwide scientific student competitions organised by the IPN – the ScienceOlympiads. In addition to the selection competitions for the international Olympiads in Biology (IBO),
Chemistry (IChO) and Physics (IPhO), they also include the International Junior Science Olympiad (IJSO), the European Olympiad of Experimental Science (EOES), and the Federal Environmental Competition (BUW). Together they address students from the beginning of secondary school until after the end of school, and, through close networking, offer the possibility of lasting support of scientific abilities and interests.
