$$A\quad F = \frac{\pi^2\,\hbar\,c}{240\,d^3}\,A \qquad B\quad F = \frac{\pi^2\,\hbar\,c}{240\,d^3}\,A^2 \qquad C\quad F = \frac{\pi^2\,\hbar\,c}{240\,d^4}\,A \qquad D\quad F = \frac{\pi^2\,\hbar\,c}{240\,d^4}\,A^2$$
