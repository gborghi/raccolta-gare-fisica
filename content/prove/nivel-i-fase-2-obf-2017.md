The gravitational field of a celestial body is the region of space that surrounds it, so that test bodies when placed in this region are attracted by the center of mass of this celestial body generating the field. Under these conditions, we launch an object vertically upwards from the Earth's surface, reaching a maximum height of $10\ \text{m}$. Se repetirmos esta experiência na Lua, onde a gravidade é $\dfrac{1}{6}$ da terrestre e com a mesma velocidade de lançamento, o objeto atingirá uma altura $H$. Determine the value of $H$.
