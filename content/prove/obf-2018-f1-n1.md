To explain the process of energy conservation and transfer between bodies, a person performs three activities on a body of mass $m$, transferring the energies $E_1$, $E_2$ and $E_3$ to it, respectively:

- I. Lifting the body to a height of $1{,}0\ \text{m}$ above the ground  energy $E_1 = mgh = 10m\ \text{J}$;
- II. Throw the body from rest on a horizontal plane without friction, varying its speed to $1{,}0\ \text{m/s}$  energy $E_2 = \tfrac{1}{2}mv^2 = 0{,}5m\ \text{J}$;
- The third. Apply a constant force to it, producing a displacement of $1{,}0\ \text{m}$ over a horizontal plane without friction, with a constant acceleration of $1{,}0\ \text{m/s}^2$  energy $E_3 = F \cdot d = ma \cdot d = m\ \text{J}$.

Identify the alternative that best expresses the relationship between the energies transferred to the body during these activities:

- **A.** $E_1 > E_3 > E_2$
- **B.** $E_3 > E_2 > E_1$
- **C.** $E_3 > E_1 > E_2$
- **D.** $E_1 > E_2 > E_3$
- **E.** $E_2 > E_3 > E_1$
