The following diagram shows the four stages of a rocket launch:

- i) At $t = 0$ the rocket engine is actuated producing a constant force at the vertical of 100 N. The mass of the rocket is 2.0 kg and it is practically unchanged throughout the flight;
- (ii) At $t = 2\,\text{s}$ the engine is turned off;
- (iii) At the maximum height the parachute (of negligible mass) is opened;
- (iv) The speed of the rocket is constant during descent with the aid of the parachute.
