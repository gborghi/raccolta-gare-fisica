**Questão 8 –** Um veículo, viajando em linha reta do ponto A ao ponto B, está com um problema no motor que causa vazamento de óleo. Uma gota de óleo cai do motor a cada $3\ \text{s}$, e a figura abaixo mostra o padrão das marcas de óleo no asfalto da rodovia. The marks are located at the following positions (in metres from A) corresponding to $t = 0,\ 3,\ 6,\ 9,\ 12,\ 15,\ 18\ \text{s}$:

$$0\ \text{m},\quad 30\ \text{m},\quad 60\ \text{m},\quad 120\ \text{m},\quad 210\ \text{m},\quad 330\ \text{m},\quad 420\ \text{m}$$

(a) What is the average speed of the car between $0$ and $18\ \text{s}$?

(b) What is the mean acceleration between $0$ and $6\ \text{s}$?

(c) What is the mean acceleration between $12$ and $18\ \text{s}$?
