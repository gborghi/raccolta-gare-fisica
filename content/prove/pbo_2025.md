**Spinning electrons (4 points)**

Danka was working in the lab with a flask filled with low-pressure argon, into which a collimated electron beam was introduced. Danka adjusted the beam so that its trajectory took the shape of a helix with a radius of $5.00\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a constant pitch of $5.00\,\mathrm{cm}$. The accelerating voltage of the electrons was $200\,\mathrm{V}$. What was the magnitude of the magnetic induction (magnetic field) in the flask produced by the external magnets under these conditions? Assume that the magnetic induction is constant throughout the entire volume of the flask and has a uniform direction.

**Risposta:** $B = 0.942\,\mathrm{mT}$
