Problema 1
De la tierra a la luna, de Julio Verne.
En la novela De la tierra a la luna de Julio Verne (Nantes, 8 de febrero de 1828-
Amiens, 24 de marzo de 1905) se narranlas peripecias de los miembros del Club
de cañones de Baltimore, condenados a la inactividad después de terminar la
guerra civil de Norteamérica, para enviar a la luna una bala de cañón tripulada.
Para esto, los miembros del club decidieron construir un cañón colosal, cargarlo
con un proyectil hueco y, por medio de un disparo, lanzar este vagón-proyectil a
la luna.
El cañón construido tenía una longitud de 200 m y estaba enterrado
verticalmente en la tierra. Como vagón-proyectil se utilizó una esfera hueca de
aluminio de 4.60 m diámetro exterior y 0.30 m de espesor. La masa total del
vagón-proyectil, teniendo en cuenta la tripulación y la carga, era de 8000 kg.
Para efectuar el disparo, el cañón se cargó con 160000 kg de algodón pólvora.
En la novela se indica que el proyectil alcanzó, al momento de salir de la boca
del cañón, una velocidad de 16 km/s y que, debido al rozamiento con el aire de
la atmósfera terrestre, la velocidad disminuyó a 11 km/s al momento de
abandonar la misma.
Se propone realizar algunos cálculos y sacar algunas conclusiones.
a) Determinar la cantidad de energía liberada por kilogramo de algodón
pólvora. Suponer que no hay rozamiento entre el proyectil y el ánima del
cañón y que se pierde un 20% de energía por calor.
b) Calcular la aceleración que experimentan los pasajeros del vagónproyectil cuando recorren el ánima del cañón. Suponer que el
movimiento del vagón dentro del ánima del cañón es uniformemente
acelerado.
c) Estudios muestran que el cuerpo humano es capaz de soportar, por
cortos períodos de tiempo, 10 veces la aceleración de la gravedad sin
perjuicio para la salud. Determinar cuál debería ser la longitud del cañón
para que la tripulación sobreviva al disparo considerando la misma
velocidad de salida del caso anterior. Tomar como valor de aceleración
de la gravedad $g = 10\ \text{m s}^{-2}$.
d) Establecer si el proyectil escapa del campo gravitatorio terrestre.
Despreciar la atracción gravitatoria de la luna.
NOTA: Entendemos por “velocidad de escape” a la velocidad inicial requerida
para ir desde un punto inicial en un campo gravitatorio (en nuestro caso el punto
inicial es el límite exterior de la atmósfera) y llegar al infinito con velocidad cero.
Cuando el vagón-proyectil se encuentre en el espacio exterior recibirá radiación
solar. Esta radiación será absorbida por el vagón-proyectil elevando su
temperatura. El flujo de radiación que recibirá el vagón-proyectil es
aproximadamente de $1366\ \dfrac{\text{W}}{\text{m}^2}$.
Considerando que el vagón-proyectil se comporta como un cuerpo negro, éste
irradiará una potencia $P$ dada por la siguiente fórmula:

$$P = \sigma A T^4$$

Donde $\sigma$ es la constante de Stefan-Boltzmann, $A$ es la superficie por la que se
emite la radiación (superficie del cuerpo) y $T$ es la temperatura del cuerpo en
Kelvin.

Dalla Terra alla Luna, di Jules Verne.
Nel romanzo Dalla Terra alla Luna di Jules Verne (Nantes, 8 febbraio 1828 -
Amiens, 24 marzo 1905) si narrano le peripezie dei membri del Gun Club di Baltimora, condannati all'inattività dopo la fine della guerra civile nordamericana, per inviare sulla Luna un proiettile di cannone con equipaggio.
A tal fine, i membri del club decisero di costruire un cannone colossale, caricarlo con un proiettile cavo e, mediante uno sparo, lanciare questo vagone-proiettile verso la Luna.
Il cannone costruito aveva una lunghezza di 200 m ed era interrato verticalmente nel terreno. Come vagone-proiettile fu utilizzata una sfera cava di alluminio di 4,60 m di diametro esterno e 0,30 m di spessore. La massa totale del vagone-proiettile, tenendo conto dell'equipaggio e del carico, era di 8000 kg.
Per effettuare lo sparo, il cannone fu caricato con 160000 kg di cotone polvere.
Nel romanzo si indica che il proiettile raggiunse, al momento di uscire dalla bocca del cannone, una velocità di 16 km/s e che, a causa dell'attrito con l'aria dell'atmosfera terrestre, la velocità diminuì a 11 km/s al momento di abbandonarla.
Si propone di eseguire alcuni calcoli e trarre alcune conclusioni.

b) Calcolare l'accelerazione che subiscono i passeggeri del vagone-proiettile quando percorrono l'anima del cannone. Supporre che il moto del vagone all'interno dell'anima del cannone sia uniformemente accelerato.
c) Studi mostrano che il corpo umano è in grado di sopportare, per brevi periodi di tempo, 10 volte l'accelerazione di gravità senza danni per la salute. Determinare quale dovrebbe essere la lunghezza del cannone affinché l'equipaggio sopravviva allo sparo considerando la stessa velocità di uscita del caso precedente. Prendere come valore dell'accelerazione di gravità $g = 10\ \text{m s}^{-2}$.

Problem 1
From the Earth to the Moon, by Jules Verne.
In the novel From the Earth to the Moon by Jules Verne (Nantes, February 8, 1828-
Amiens, March 24, 1905) the adventures of the members of the Baltimore Gun Club are narrated, condemned to inactivity after the end of the North American Civil War, to send a manned cannonball to the moon.
For this, the club members decided to build a colossal cannon, load it with a hollow projectile and, by means of a shot, launch this projectile-car to the moon.
The cannon built had a length of 200 m and was buried vertically in the ground. As the projectile-car, a hollow aluminum sphere with an outer diameter of 4.60 m and a thickness of 0.30 m was used. The total mass of the projectile-car, taking into account the crew and the cargo, was 8000 kg.
To fire the shot, the cannon was loaded with 160000 kg of gun cotton.
In the novel it is stated that the projectile reached, at the moment of leaving the mouth of the cannon, a speed of 16 km/s and that, due to friction with the air of the Earth's atmosphere, the speed decreased to 11 km/s at the moment of leaving it.
It is proposed to carry out some calculations and draw some conclusions.
a) Determine the amount of energy released per kilogram of gun cotton. Assume that there is no friction between the projectile and the bore of the cannon and that 20% of energy is lost as heat.
b) Calculate the acceleration experienced by the passengers of the projectile car when they travel through the barrel of the cannon. Assume that the motion of the car inside the cannon barrel is uniformly accelerated.
c) Studies show that the human body is capable of withstanding, for short periods of time, 10 times the acceleration of gravity without harm to health. Determine what the length of the cannon should be so that the crew survives the shot, considering the same muzzle velocity as in the previous case. Take as the value of the acceleration of gravity $g = 10\ \text{m s}^{-2}$.
d) Establish whether the projectile escapes the Earth's gravitational field.
Neglect the gravitational attraction of the moon.
NOTE: By "escape velocity" we mean the initial velocity required to go from an initial point in a gravitational field (in our case the initial point is the outer limit of the atmosphere) and reach infinity with zero velocity.
When the projectile car is in outer space it will receive solar radiation. This radiation will be absorbed by the projectile car, raising its temperature. The radiation flux that the projectile car will receive is approximately $1366\ \dfrac{\text{W}}{\text{m}^2}$.
Considering that the projectile car behaves as a black body, it will radiate a power $P$ given by the following formula:

$$P = \sigma A T^4$$

Where $\sigma$ is the Stefan-Boltzmann constant, $A$ is the surface through which the radiation is emitted (surface of the body) and $T$ is the temperature of the body in
Kelvin.

To have lighting, the projectile-car has a system that transforms part of the energy received, in the form of radiation, into electrical energy. Qué tubooo!!!

Marco teórico
Un tubo de Kundt es un dispositivo que permite estudiar ondas de sonido
estacionarias. El dispositivo consiste de un tubo cilíndrico, cerrado por ambos
extremos. En uno de los extremos se ubica un emisor de ondas sonoras de una
dada frecuencia ($f$) y en el otro extremo, un detector de las mismas. El
funcionamiento del tubo de Kundt se basa en la generación de ondas
estacionarias en la región comprendida entre el emisor y el detector. Las ondas
estacionarias se producen al superponerse dos ondas de igual amplitud y
frecuencia ($f$), que viajan paralelas al eje del tubo, una de ellas en el sentido
emisor-detector, y la otra en el sentido opuesto (esto es, en el sentido detectoremisor). Esta última onda resulta de la reflexión de la primera onda (generada
por el emisor) en el extremo del tubo donde se encuentra ubicado el detector.

Las ondas estacionarias se caracterizan por la existencia de zonas donde la
onda tiene amplitud máxima (máximos o vientres) y otras con amplitud cero
(nodos), las cuales tienen posiciones constantes en el tiempo. El número de
nodos y máximos que se establecen dentro del tubo de Kundt depende de la
longitud del mismo y de la frecuencia de la onda emitida; sin embargo la
velocidad de propagación ($v$) de la onda permanece constante. La distancia
existente entre dos nodos consecutivos, o entre dos máximos consecutivos,
siempre es igual a media longitud de onda.
La velocidad de propagación de la onda está relacionada con su longitud de
onda $\lambda$ y con su frecuencia $f$ por la expresión:

$$v = f\,\lambda \qquad [1]$$

Entonces, en base a la ecuación [1], un tubo de Kundt permite realizar
mediciones para determinar la velocidad del sonido.

Un tubo di Kundt è un dispositivo che permette di studiare onde sonore stazionarie. Il dispositivo consiste di un tubo cilindrico, chiuso a entrambe le estremità. Theoretical framework
A Kundt's tube is a device that allows the study of stationary sound waves. The device consists of a cylindrical tube, closed at both ends. At one of the ends, a sound wave emitter of a given frequency ($f$) is located, and at the other end, a detector for the same waves. The operation of the Kundt's tube is based on the generation of stationary waves in the region between the emitter and the detector. Stationary waves are produced when two waves of equal amplitude and frequency ($f$) are superimposed, traveling parallel to the axis of the tube, one of them in the emitter-detector direction, and the other in the opposite direction (that is, in the detector-emitter direction). This latter wave results from the reflection of the first wave (generated by the emitter) at the end of the tube where the detector is located.

Standing waves are characterized by the existence of regions where the wave has maximum amplitude (maxima or antinodes) and others with zero amplitude (nodes), which have constant positions over time. The number of nodes and maxima that are established inside the Kundt's tube depends on its length and on the frequency of the emitted wave; however, the propagation speed ($v$) of the wave remains constant. The distance between two consecutive nodes, or between two consecutive maxima, is always equal to half a wavelength.
The propagation speed of the wave is related to its wavelength $\lambda$ and to its frequency $f$ by the expression:

$$v = f\,\lambda \qquad [1]$$

Therefore, based on equation [1], a Kundt's tube allows measurements to be made to determine the speed of sound.

Emitter
The emitter is a device that generates sound waves in the frequency range between 37000 Hz and 42000 Hz. The emitter is connected to an electronic circuit that allows the frequency of the ultrasound wave to be varied.
The frequency of the generated wave can be modified by varying the value of the resistor that is connected to it.
The relationship between the frequency and the value of the resistor is given by the following formula:

$$f = \dfrac{1}{b + a R} \qquad [2]$$

where $f$ is given in kilo Hertz ($\text{kHz}$) and $R$ in kilo Ohm ($\text{k}\Omega$).

Detector
It is a device that transforms the amplitude of ultrasound waves into a continuous electrical voltage signal. This voltage can be measured with a multimeter set to voltmeter mode (DCV) (See Multimeter Use in Appendix).

Operation
When setting a value of the resistance connected to the wave generator, the emitter emits an ultrasound wave whose frequency f is determined by equation [2], and standing waves are established in the cylindrical cavity.
By changing the position of the piston, the length of the cavity is varied; when the distance ($L^*$) separating the emitter from the detector is an integer number of half-wavelengths, the so-called resonance condition is met, that is, when:

$$L^* = n\,\dfrac{\lambda}{2} \qquad (n\ \text{es: } 1,2,3,4,\ldots) \qquad [3]$$

Therefore, if $L^*$ is changed, the number of nodes and maxima inside the cavity changes. This causes the signal recorded by the detector to vary, reaching maximum or minimum values, depending on whether the detector position corresponds to a maximum or a node of the standing wave.
