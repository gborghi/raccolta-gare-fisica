in cui $m(n) = 1.008665\ \text{u}$, $m(\text{U}) = 235.043915\ \text{u}$, $m(\text{Ba}) = 140.9139\ \text{u}$, $m(\text{Kr}) = 91.8973\ \text{u}$ e $u = 1.66 \times 10^{-27}\ \text{kg}$.

- **(A)** 1.0
- **(B)** 3.5
- **(C)** 2.3
- **(D)** 4.6
- **(E)** 0.1
