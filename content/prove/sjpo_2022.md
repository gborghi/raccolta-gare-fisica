A slab of mass $m_1$ rests on a frictionless floor, and a block of mass $m_2$ rests on top of the slab. Between the block and slab, the coefficient of static friction is $\mu_s$ and the coefficient of kinetic friction is $\mu_k$. A horizontal force of magnitude $F$ begins to pull directly on the block, as shown. The condition for the two masses to move as one piece is ___, where $M = m_1 + m_2$.

![[SJPO_2022_p12_f1.png]]

- **(A)** $\mu_s \le \dfrac{m_1}{m_2}\dfrac{F}{Mg}$
- **(B)** $\mu_s \le \dfrac{m_2}{m_1}\dfrac{F}{Mg}$
- **(C)** $\mu_s \ge \dfrac{m_1}{m_2}\dfrac{F}{Mg}$
