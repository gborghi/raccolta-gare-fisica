**(a)** A compound pendulum consists of a uniform thin rod $AB$ of length $50\ \text{cm}$ and mass $1.5\ \text{kg}$ and a thin disc with diameter $15\ \text{cm}$ and mass $3.0\ \text{kg}$ attached to the end $B$ of the rod. *[8 marks]*

*[Moment of inertia of a disc, with mass $M$ and radius $R$, about an axis through its centre is $\tfrac{1}{2}MR^2$; Moment of inertia of a thin rod, with mass $M$ and length $\ell$, about an axis through its end is $\tfrac{1}{3}M\ell^2$.]*

*[5 marks]*

*[kgf = kilogram-force. It is the weight of a kilogram under standard gravity. One $\text{kgf} = 9.81\ \text{N}$. Densities (in $\text{kg m}^{-3}$): gold: $19\,300$; copper: $8\,960$.]*

**(b)** The surface charge density $\sigma(r)$ at a point on a circular thin disc, having diameter $20\ \text{cm}$, can be described by the equation

$$\sigma(r) = 1.05 + r \quad \mu\text{C m}^{-2}$$

where $r$ is the distance of the point from the centre of the disc. A point charge of $+10\ \text{nC}$ is placed on the axis of the disc and at a distance $50\ \text{cm}$ from the centre of the disc. *[7 marks]*

*[For a uniformly charged ring with radius $R$, and carrying a total charge of $+Q$, the electric field on the axial point, at a distance $x$ from the centre of the ring is*
$$\vec{E} = \frac{Q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\,\frac{x}{\left(R^2 + x^2\right)^{3/2}}\,\hat{\imath}$$
*where $\hat{\imath}$ is a unit vector along the axis of the ring and pointing away from the centre of the ring.]*
