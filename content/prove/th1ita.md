1. Image charge in a metallic object
Introduction – Method of images

A point particle with electric charge is placed near a grounded spherical metallic surface of radius
[see Fig. 1(a)]. As a result, a surface charge distribution is induced on the sphere. Calculating the electric field and electrostatic potential produced by this surface charge distribution is a highly complicated task. However, this calculation can be significantly simplified using a mathematical technique known as the method of images. In this method, the electric field and electrostatic potential produced by the charge distribution on the sphere are equivalent to those generated by a single point charge located inside the sphere (you do not need to prove this). Note: The electric field and electrostatic potential of this image charge reproduce the actual electric field and electrostatic potential only outside the sphere (including its surface).
