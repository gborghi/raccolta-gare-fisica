(d) It is reasonable to suppose that the current went up one leg of the man, across the torso (and the heart), and down through the other leg. (e) Using your expression for E(r), obtain the potential difference, ΔV, between the tip of the rod and a point situated at distance r. Take the tip of the rod to have radius a. (f) Currents ranging from 0.10A to 1.0A across a torso can trigger fibrillation in the heart. Could the accident have been caused by the current that leaked from the rod?
