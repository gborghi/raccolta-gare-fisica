Thus imaging using radioisotopes can be used to indicate malignant tissue growth. Technetium-99m (99mTc) is one such radioisotope, which is used for imaging organs such as the thyroid, liver, kidneys, and brain. The half-life, the interval during which the number of atoms left of a radioisotope halves, of 99mTc is τ1/2​=6.01 hours. Rates of radioactivity can be measured in Curies (Ci), equivalent to 3.7×1010 decays per second.
