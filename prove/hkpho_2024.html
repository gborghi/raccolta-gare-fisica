(b) [2] What is the minimum initial velocity u such that the ball can reach point B?

(c) [3] How long does the ball take to reach point B?

Define the second cosmic velocity v2​, also known as the escape velocity from the Earth, as the least speed that a projectile launched at sea level must have to escape from the Earth (i.e. reach infinite distance from the Earth when the only force acting on it is the Earth’s gravity). Define the third cosmic velocity v3​ as the least speed that a projectile launched at sea level must have to escape from the solar system; it is defined relative to the Earth (not the Sun), and the required speed is minimum when the projectile is launched in the same direction as the Earth’s orbital velocity.

(iii) [2] What is the least vP′′​ for P to escape from the solar system?

(iv) [4] Hence calculate v3​, taking into account that ME​≫m (note that v3​ is defined as the speed relative to the Earth).
