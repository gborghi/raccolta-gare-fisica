t=f(u)

(c) [4 marks] Qualitatively plot the rate of change of temperature (dT/dt) vs T. Mark any significant point(s) on the temperature axis in the plot.

(d) [4 marks] At what time t does the temperature T of the gas reach 20K? What is the piston velocity (vp​) at this point?
