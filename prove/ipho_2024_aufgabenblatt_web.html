Problem 3 (10 points) Helicopter on Mars On 19 April 2021, a human-developed aircraft took off on Mars for the first time. The Ingenuity helicopter has since completed numerous flights in the Martian atmosphere. During the development of Ingenuity on Earth, the conditions in the Martian atmosphere had to be taken into account. On Earth, whose atmosphere near the ground has a density of 1.2 kg m−3, the helicopter took off at 500 rotor revolutions per minute. The density of the Martian atmosphere near the ground, by contrast, is only about 0.020 kg m−3.
