PROBLEM no. 1 – Equilibrismi… (100 Points)

Question no. 1.

In the absence of friction, the forces acting on the block of mass m2​ are: its weight (vertical), the normal reaction force N from the horizontal plane (vertical), the normal reaction force N′ due to the block of mass m1​ (oblique), and the force F (horizontal).

The forces acting on the block of mass m1​ are: its weight (vertical), the normal reaction force N′′ from the vertical plane (horizontal), and the normal reaction force −N′ due to the block of mass m2​ (oblique).

All’equilibrio le forze su ciascun blocco devono avere risultante nulla. In componenti, prendendo l’asse x orizzontale da sinistra a destra e l’asse y verticale dal basso verso l’alto, e indicando con F0​ l’intensità di F in condizioni di equilibrio, si ha:

F0​−N′senθ=0 N−m2​g−N′cosθ=0 N′senθ−N′′=0 N′cosθ−m1​g=0

N′=cosθm1​g​F0​=m1​gtanθ=28.3N

Question no. 2.

At equilibrium, the net force on each block must be zero. In components, taking the x-axis horizontal from left to right and the y-axis vertical from bottom to top, and denoting by F0​ the magnitude of F under equilibrium conditions, one obtains:

F0​−N′sinθ=0 N−m2​g−N′cosθ=0 N′sinθ−N′′=0 N′cosθ−m1​g=0

From the first and fourth of these equations, by simple substitutions, one obtains:

N′=cosθm1​g​F0​=m1​gtanθ=28.3N

Quesito n. 3.

Con una forza maggiore, pari a 2F0​, non si ha più equilibrio; il corpo di massa m2​ si muove con accelerazione a2​ orizzontale e il corpo di massa m1​ si muove con accelerazione a1​ verticale. Le equazioni precedenti, pertanto, si modificano così:

2F0​−N′senθ=m2​a2​ N−m2​g−N′cosθ=0 N′senθ−N′′=0 N′cosθ−m1​g=m1​a1​

Inoltre, per considerazioni geometriche, fra gli spostamenti, e quindi le accelerazioni, dei due blocchi vale la relazione a1​:a2​=senθ:cosθ, cioè a2​=a1​/tanθ. Sostituendo nella prima e quarta equazione qui sopra, si ricava:

Question no. 3.

With a greater force, equal to 2F0​, equilibrium is no longer maintained; the body of mass m2​ moves with horizontal acceleration a2​, and the body of mass m1​ moves with vertical acceleration a1​. The previous equations therefore change as follows:

2F0​−N′sinθ=m2​a2​ N−m2​g−N′cosθ=0 N′sinθ−N′′=0 N′cosθ−m1​g=m1​a1​

Moreover, from geometric considerations, the relationship between the displacements, and therefore the accelerations, of the two blocks is a1​:a2​=sinθ:cosθ, that is a2​=a1​/tanθ. Substituting into the first and fourth equations above, we obtain:

N′=sinθ2F0​​−sinθtanθm2​a1​​=cosθ2m1​g​−sinθtanθm2​a1​​ a1​=m1​N′cosθ​−g=g−m1​tan2θm2​a1​​ a1​=gm1​tan2θ+m2​m1​tan2θ​=3.50ms−2

Quesito n. 4.

Il blocco di massa m2​ ha un’accelerazione a2​=a1​/tanθ e pertanto, a partire dall’istante t=0 in cui la forza F raddoppia l’intensità, ha una velocità v=a2​t. La potenza da applicare, pertanto, è W=2F0​v=2m1​ga1​t. Essa cresce linearmente nel tempo con un coefficiente 2m1​ga1​=344Ws−1.

Question no. 4.

The block of mass m2​ has an acceleration a2​=a1​/tanθ and therefore, starting from the instant t=0 when the force F doubles in magnitude, it has a velocity v=a2​t. The power to be applied is thus W=2F0​v=2m1​ga1​t. It increases linearly with time, with a coefficient 2m1​ga1​=344Ws−1.

Note: Substituting a1​ with the already rounded value, as reported above in point 3, would yield W/t=343Ws−1, which is equally acceptable.

Quesito n. 5.

In presenza di forze di attrito, A e A′′ (rispettivamente), sulle superfici orizzontale e verticale che si aggiungono alle altre forze già considerate, la forza F minima per ottenere l’equilibrio dev’essere minore di F0​, per cui l’attrito orizzontale dev’essere rivolto nel verso positivo delle x per ostacolare il moto del blocco di massa m2​ verso sinistra, e quello verticale verso l’alto per ostacolare il moto del blocco di massa m1​ verso il basso, moti che si avrebbero in assenza di F e di attriti.

Quindi le equazioni precedenti si modificano così:

F+A−N′senθ=0 N−m2​g−N′cosθ=0 N′senθ−N′′=0 N′cosθ+A′′−m1​g=0

I valori massimi possibili di A e A′′ sono rispettivamente A=μN e A′′=μN′′. Indichiamo i loro valori effettivi con A=ρN e A′′=ρ′′N′′. Questa volta servono tutte e quattro le equazioni precedenti; si ricava

N′′=N′senθ N′cosθ+ρ′′N′senθ−m1​g=0⇒N′=cosθ+ρ′′senθm1​g​ N=(cosθ+ρ′′senθm1​cosθ​+m2​)g F=cosθ+ρ′′senθm1​senθ−ρ(m1​+m2​)cosθ−ρρ′′m2​senθ​g

Il valore di F decresce in modo monotono al crescere sia di ρ che di ρ′′, per cui per minimizzare F (finché esso è positivo, naturalmente) occorre assumere i valori massimi possibili sia di ρ che di ρ′′. L’ultima formula diviene pertanto:

Fmin​=cosθ+μsenθm1​senθ−μ(m1​+m2​)cosθ−μ2m2​senθ​g=10.7N

Question no. 5.

In the presence of friction forces, A and A′′ (respectively) on the horizontal and vertical surfaces that are added to the other forces already considered, the minimum force F required to achieve equilibrium must be less than F0​, so that the horizontal friction must act in the positive direction of x to hinder the motion of the block of mass m2​ toward the left, and the vertical friction must act upward to hinder the motion of the block of mass m1​ downward, motions that would occur in the absence of F and friction.

Therefore, the previous equations are modified as follows:

F+A−N′sinθ=0 N−m2​g−N′cosθ=0 N′sinθ−N′′=0 N′cosθ+A′′−m1​g=0

The maximum possible values of A and A′′ are respectively A=μN and A′′=μN′′. We denote their actual values by A=ρN and A′′=ρ′′N′′. This time all four of the previous equations are needed; it follows that

N′′=N′sinθ N′cosθ+ρ′′N′sinθ−m1​g=0⇒N′=cosθ+ρ′′sinθm1​g​ N=(cosθ+ρ′′sinθm1​cosθ​+m2​)g F=cosθ+ρ′′sinθm1​sinθ−ρ(m1​+m2​)cosθ−ρρ′′m2​sinθ​g

The value of F decreases monotonically with increasing ρ and ρ′′, so to minimize F (as long as it remains positive, naturally) one must assume the maximum possible values for both ρ and ρ′′. The last formula therefore becomes:

Fmin​=cosθ+μsinθm1​sinθ−μ(m1​+m2​)cosθ−μ2m2​sinθ​g=10.7N

PROBLEM no. 2 – Watch out for inductances! (50 Points)

Question no. 1.

At the closing of the switch, the current through the inductance remains instantaneously zero and the equation for the active mesh gives

3RI0​=E⇒E=3(2.5kΩ)(5mA)=37.5V

In steady state, instead, the electromotive force across the inductance is zero, so the resistance seen by the generator is R∗=R+(R2​∥R1​), hence the equation for the equivalent circuit gives

R∗I1​=E⇒(R+2R+R1​2RR1​​)I1​=2R+R1​2R2+3RR1​​I1​=2R+R1​2R+3R1​​RI1​=E

which, substituting the previous expression for E, reduces to

(2R+3R1​)I1​=3(2R+R1​)I0​⇒3(I1​−I0​)R1​=2(3I0​−I1​)R

from which

R1​=3(I1​−I0​)2(3I0​−I1​)​R=3(9−5)mA2(15−9)mA​R=R=2.5kΩ

Question no. 2.

The magnetic energy stored in the circuit is UL​=21​LIL2​, where IL​ is the current in the inductance branch. At steady state, the voltage across the inductance is zero and thus it suffices to consider the parallel combination of the resistances R1​=R and R2​=2R: it immediately follows that the current in R1​ is double the other and equal to 2/3 of the total current.

More formally, this current is determined by the equations (known as the current divider equations):

{IL​+IR2​​=I1​RIL​=2RIR2​​​⇒IL​=3R2R​I1​=32​I1​=6mA

Immediately after the switch is reopened, the magnetic energy is dissipated by the resistances R1​ and R2​, in parts proportional to their resistance values, since the current is the same at every point of the loop: thus W1​=R1​I2; W2​=R2​I2.

Therefore, in resistance R1​, one-third of the stored energy is dissipated: hence

U=31​UL​=31​21​LIL2​⇒L=IL2​6U​=6.67mH

Question no. 1.

The energy flux entering the apartment, in the form of heat due to conduction, is given by Φin​=K(Te​−T). The energy flux extracted by the air conditioner from the apartment is given by Φout​=αεP, where ε is the efficiency of an ideal refrigerator (i.e., a refrigerator that uses the Carnot cycle). Since the air conditioner operates between two reservoirs, the apartment at temperature T and the external environment at temperature Te​, the efficiency of a Carnot refrigerator operating between the same reservoirs is given by ε=T/(Te​−T), so that, substituting back into the expression for the outgoing flux, one obtains

Φout​=αTe​−TT​P
