Question no. 1.

The figure below shows the system of forces acting on the three cylinders labeled as no. 1, 2 and 3: its symbols are referred to in the following.

NOTE: for clarity reasons, the magnitudes of the forces in this figure are not drawn to scale.

The following figures show the free-body diagrams for cylinder no. 3 and cylinder no. 1 (the diagram for cylinder no. 2 is symmetric to the second one). The forces N1​ and N2​, and their reactions, N1′​ and N2′​ (which, by the third law of dynamics, are respectively equal in magnitude to the first ones and opposite in direction), are inclined at 60∘ with respect to the horizontal plane because the triangle whose vertices are the centers of the circular cross-sections of the cylinders in contact with each other is clearly equilateral; it follows that their horizontal and vertical components are obtained by multiplying the magnitudes respectively by cos60∘=1/2 and sin60∘=3​/2.

Let P=Mg​ be the weight of each cylinder. The equilibrium conditions for cylinder no. 3, taking into account that by symmetry N1​=N2​, lead to the following equation, related to the vertical component of the forces

223​​N1​−P=0.

Therefore

N1​=3​P​.

For equilibrium of the system it is sufficient to study cylinder no. 1, since the force system is equivalent to that of cylinder no. 2. The condition for equilibrium in horizontal translations is sufficient to determine the tension T in each of the two strings.

2T−N3​−21​N1​=0from which2T−N3​=23​P​.

The minimum value of T occurs for N3​=0, so that

Tmin​=43​P​.

Quesito n. 2.

In assenza di attrito, poiché le rette d’azione di tutte le forze presenti tra i cilindri e tra il piano e i cilindri intersecano gli assi di ciascun cilindro, tutti i momenti assiali sono nulli; se dunque inizialmente i cilindri sono in quiete, non si avrà nessun moto rotatorio: i tre cilindri traslano senza rotolare.

Poiché si assume che il sistema accelerato non collassi, esso si muove rigidamente e dunque, per il secondo principio, l’accelerazione a può essere subito determinata essendo F=3Ma.

Si considerano poi le equazioni di moto, separatamente per ciascun cilindro (per i simboli fare riferimento ai relativi diagrammi di corpo libero), come segue. Rispetto al caso statico studiato prima, il sistema di forze non è più simmetrico: adesso le reazioni vincolari non hanno lo stesso modulo, mentre, per il principio di azione e reazione, resta vero che N1​=N1′​, N2​=N2′​, N3​=N3′​.

Per il cilindro n. 1

{F−N3​−21​N1​=MaN4​−P−23​​N1​=0​

Per il cilindro n. 2

{N3′​+21​N2​=MaN5​−P−23​​N2​=0​

Per il cilindro n. 3

{21​N1′​−21​N2′​=Ma23​​N2′​+23​​N1′​−P=0​

L’insieme delle 6 equazioni costituiscono un sistema nelle incognite N1​,...,5 ed a. Quest’ultima, anche senza la considerazione fatta sopra, si sarebbe potuta trovare sommando membro a membro le tre equazioni relative alle componenti orizzontali del moto (le prime di ogni coppia), per cui si può sostituire Ma=F/3

N3​=3F​−2N2​​=2F​−23​P​

Anche in questo caso deve essere N3​>0. Da qui si ricava F>3​P​.

In conclusione, il sistema non collassa se viene accelerato nel modo descritto da una forza F tale che

3​Mg​<F<3​Mg.

PROBLEMA n. 2 – Tri-ciclo … termodinamico (35 Punti)

Il rendimento η di una macchina termica è dato dal rapporto tra il lavoro netto scambiato nel ciclo e il calore assorbito. Il lavoro si calcola come segue.

Nell’isoterma quasi statica 1–2, essendo V2​=2V1​, il lavoro è dato da:

L12​=∫V1​V2​​p(V)dV=∫V1​V2​​VnRT​dV=nRTln(V1​V2​​)=p1​V1​ln2

Nel raffreddamento isobaro quasi statico 2–3, tenendo conto del fatto che p2​=p1​/2 e V3​=V1​, il lavoro è:

L23​=∫V2​V3​​p2​dV=p2​(V3​−V2​)=−21​p1​V1​

Infine, nell’isocora 2–3, il lavoro è nullo. Il lavoro netto del ciclo risulta quindi:

Lciclo​=p1​V1​(ln2−21​)

Si calcola ora il calore assorbito nel ciclo: il sistema assorbe calore nell’espansione isoterma 1–2 e nel riscaldamento isocoro 3–1.

Nell’espansione isoterma, poiché l’energia interna di un gas perfetto dipende solo dalla temperatura, ΔU=0, e dunque Q12​=L12​.

Nel riscaldamento isocoro, L=0, e dunque Q=ΔU=ncV​ΔT.

Poiché il gas è monoatomico, cV​=(3/2)R e risulta

Q31​=23​nRΔT

Dall’equazione di stato dei gas perfetti, tenendo conto che il volume non varia, si ha VΔp=nRΔT e dunque

Q31​=23​V1​Δp=43​V1​p1​

In definitiva:

Qass​=Q12​+Q31​=p1​V1​(ln2+43​)

Infine il rendimento risulta:

η=Qass​Lciclo​​=ln2+3/4ln2−1/2​=0.134≡13.4%

Question no. 2.

In the absence of friction, since the lines of action of all forces present between the cylinders and between the plane and the cylinders intersect the axes of each cylinder, all axial torques are zero; therefore, if initially the cylinders are at rest, no rotational motion will occur: the three cylinders translate without rolling.

Since it is assumed that the accelerated system does not collapse, it moves rigidly and thus, by Newton’s second law, the acceleration a can be immediately determined as being F=3Ma.

The equations of motion are then considered separately for each cylinder (for the symbols, refer to the corresponding free-body diagrams), as follows. Compared to the static case studied previously, the force system is no longer symmetric: now the constraint reactions do not have the same magnitude, while, by Newton’s third law, it remains true that N1​=N1′​, N2​=N2′​, N3​=N3′​.

For cylinder no. 1

{F−N3​−21​N1​=MaN4​−P−23​​N1​=0​

For cylinder no. 2

{N3′​+21​N2​=MaN5​−P−23​​N2​=0​

For cylinder no. 3

{21​N1′​−21​N2′​=Ma23​​N2′​+23​​N1′​−P=0​

The set of six equations constitutes a system in the unknowns N1​,...,5 and a. The latter could have been found even without the consideration above by summing term by term the three equations relative to the horizontal components of motion (the first in each pair), so that Ma=F/3 may be substituted.

The force system on cylinder no. 3 allows N1​ and N2​ to be determined. Indeed, solving the system yields

N1​=3​P​+3F​N2​=3​P​−3F​

A first condition on F is thus obtained by observing that the system does not collapse if N2​>0. From this, it follows that F<3​P.

The second condition on F is obtained from the equations of motion of one of the other two cylinders. In particular, from the horizontal component equation of motion for cylinder no. 2, one obtains

N3​=3F​−2N2​​=2F​−23​P​

In this case too, it must be N3​>0. From here, one obtains F>3​P​.

In conclusion, the system does not collapse if it is accelerated in the manner described by a force F such that

3​Mg​<F<3​Mg.

PROBLEM no. 2 – Tri-cycle … thermodynamic (35 Points)

The efficiency η of a heat engine is given by the ratio between the net work exchanged in the cycle and the heat absorbed. The work is calculated as follows.

In the quasi-static isothermal process 1–2, since V2​=2V1​, the work is given by:

L12​=∫V1​V2​​p(V)dV=∫V1​V2​​VnRT​dV=nRTln(V1​V2​​)=p1​V1​ln2

In the quasi-static isobaric cooling process 2–3, taking into account that p2​=p1​/2 and V3​=V1​, the work is:

L23​=∫V2​V3​​p2​dV=p2​(V3​−V2​)=−21​p1​V1​

Finally, in the isochoric process 2–3, the work is zero. The net work of the cycle thus results in:

Lciclo​=p1​V1​(ln2−21​)

The heat absorbed in the cycle is now computed: the system absorbs heat during the isothermal expansion 1–2 and during the isochoric heating 3–1.

During the isothermal expansion, since the internal energy of an ideal gas depends only on temperature, ΔU=0, and thus Q12​=L12​.

During the isochoric heating, L=0, and thus Q=ΔU=ncV​ΔT.

Since the gas is monatomic, cV​=(3/2)R, and one obtains

Q31​=23​nRΔT

From the ideal gas equation of state, taking into account that volume does not change, one has VΔp=nRΔT, and thus

Q31​=23​V1​Δp=43​V1​p1​

In conclusion:

Qass​=Q12​+Q31​=p1​V1​(ln2+43​)

Finally, the efficiency is:

η=Qass​Lciclo​​=ln2+3/4ln2−1/2​=0.134≡13.4%

Quesito n. 2.

Da quanto sopra si evince che per r=r0​ l’energia potenziale ha un minimo, e la forza è nulla. Questa è pertanto la distanza di equilibrio (stabile). Nell’espressione precedente della forza il primo termine è repulsivo e il secondo è attrattivo; siccome il primo termine è maggiore del secondo in valore assoluto quando r<r0​, l’interazione è repulsiva per r<r0​ e attrattiva per r>r0​. In un modello più realistico gli esponenti sarebbero più alti, ma il modello è stato qui scelto in modo da essere facilmente trattabile.

Question no. 2.

From the above it follows that for r=r0​ the potential energy has a minimum, and the force is zero. This is therefore the equilibrium distance (stable).
In the previous expression for the force, the first term is repulsive and the second is attractive; since the first term has a greater absolute value than the second when r<r0​, the interaction is repulsive for r<r0​ and attractive for r>r0​. In a more realistic model, the exponents would be higher, but the model has been chosen here to be easily manageable.

Question no. 3.

Following the application of an external force, the crystal deforms moving away from equilibrium, and each interatomic distance in the direction of the force changes from r0​ to r=r0​+δ (in directions orthogonal to the force, the interatomic distance remains r0​ because there are no interactions with second neighbors). In this way, a force is exerted that balances the external one overall.

To study small deformations, let r=r0​+δ with δ≪r0​; then terms in (δ/r0​)2 are neglected compared to those in δ/r0​. The force becomes

f=4U0​(r0​+δ)5r04​​−4U0​(r0​+δ)3r02​​

and expanding the binomials in the denominator to first order in δ/r0​, one obtains

f≈4U0​r0​(1+5δ/r0​)1​−4U0​r0​(1+3δ/r0​)1​.

In the same order of approximation, one can write that

1+5δ/r0​1​≈1−5r0​δ​and similarly for the other term.

One could have reached this expression in a single step, since (1+x)α≈1+αx with α=−5.

One thus obtains

f≈r0​4U0​​[(1−5r0​δ​)−(1−3r0​δ​)]=−r02​8U0​δ​.

Each bond is stretched by an amount δ in the direction of application of the external force, so that ΔL/L=δ/r0​. The internal forces f balance each other, and only the external forces at the edge of the crystal remain, which add up in proportion to the number n of bonds per unit area; since F=nAf and n=1/r02​, one has

AF​=A∣nf∣​=r02​∣f∣​from which it follows thatE=δ/r0​∣f/r02​∣​=r03​8U0​​.

Substituting numerical values and remembering that 1 eV=1.6×10−19 J, one obtains E=2.37×1011 N/m2. The order of magnitude obtained is reasonable (for steel it is E=2×1011 N/m2).
